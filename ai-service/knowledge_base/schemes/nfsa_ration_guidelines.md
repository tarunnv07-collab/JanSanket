# National Food Security Scheme (NFSA) Operational Guidelines

## Title: Ration Card Distribution Standards & Fair Price Shop Obligations
1. **Entitlement**: Priority Households (PHH) receive 5kg of foodgrains per person per month. Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY) households receive 35kg per family per month.
2. **Shop Availability**: Fair Price Shops (FPS) must display stock registers, beneficiary quota charts, and helpline contacts prominently at the shop entrance.
3. **Closure Violations**: If a Fair Price Shop is found closed during official operational hours without prior notification from the District Supply Officer, the license holder is subject to suspension under Clause 14(b).
4. **Grievance Resolution**: Complaints detailing non-receipt of ration for 60 days or more must trigger automatic systemic review and allocation audit across the local supply chain.
