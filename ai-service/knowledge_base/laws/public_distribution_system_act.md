# Targeted Public Distribution System (Control) Act & National Food Security Guidelines

## Section 1: Entitlement and Monthly Distribution
1. Every eligible ration cardholder under the Public Distribution System (PDS) is entitled to receive monthly quota of foodgrains (rice, wheat, coarse grains) by the 10th of every month.
2. Fair Price Shops (Ration Shops) must remain open for a minimum of 8 hours daily, 6 days a week, with visible display of working hours and stock availability boards.
3. Non-supply of ration for two consecutive months constitutes a material breach of service delivery under Section 7 of the Food Security Rules.

## Section 2: Grievance Redressal Timeline
1. Complaints regarding non-issuance or delayed distribution of foodgrains must be investigated within 7 working days by the District Supply Officer (DSO).
2. Inspection officers must physically verify store logbooks and biometric POS records upon receiving failure of supply reports.
