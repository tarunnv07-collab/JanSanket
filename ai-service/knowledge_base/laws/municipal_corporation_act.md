# Municipal Corporation Infrastructure & Service Delivery Act

## Section 1: Road Maintenance and Pothole Repair
1. The Municipal Corporation Public Works Department (PWD) is required to inspect and repair major road potholes within 48 hours of citizen complaint registration.
2. Unrepaired potholes exceeding 5 cm depth on arterial roads constitute public safety hazards under Section 42 of the Municipal Act.

## Section 2: Solid Waste and Water Supply Services
1. Garbage collection points and community dustbins must be cleared at least once per 24-hour cycle.
2. Contaminated or non-functional water pipelines affecting public residential zones must be attended to within 24 hours.
